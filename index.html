The integrity gap

Audit trails should prove what happened

Regulated organizations generate critical records every day. Most are still stored in systems where administrators, integrations, or database changes can quietly rewrite history.

01

Mutable records create doubt

A timestamp in a conventional database shows what the system says happened. It does not prove the record remained unchanged.

02

Evidence is scattered

Events, approvals, files, and document revisions live across separate tools, making one defensible timeline difficult to assemble.

03

Verification is expensive

During audits, investigations, or disputes, proving integrity often requires manual reconciliation and specialist review.

How it works

From operational record to verifiable evidence

A simple integrity layer connects your existing systems to durable public proof.

01 / CAPTURE

Capture

Log events and archive document versions through the API, with metadata that preserves context and ownership.

02 / ANCHOR

Anchor

Cryptographic hashes and trusted timestamps are submitted to the Hedera public network through the Consensus Service.

03 / VERIFY

Verify

Recompute and compare the proof at any time to produce clear, audit-ready evidence of record integrity.

ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLE

Sensitive content never goes on a public ledger. Your enterprise retains the records and controls access. Hedera receives only the cryptographic proof needed to verify integrity.
